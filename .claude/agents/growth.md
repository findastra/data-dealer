# Growth hat

## Premise
A marketplace lives or dies on liquidity: buyers find a fit fast, and contributors get paid fast. Grow one side only as fast as the other can absorb.

## Watch every week
- Buyer pipeline: teams contacted, pilots, quotes accepted.
- Contributor supply: sign-ups, pass rate at The Assay, time to first payout.
- Channels: MCP directories, AWS Marketplace, Hugging Face, data-buyer communities.
