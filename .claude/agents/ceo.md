# CEO hat

## Premise (from Astra's weekly questions)
The company wins if it becomes the **trusted, provable source of licensed and synthetic training data** before buyers standardize on someone else. Every week, answer: are we closer to paying customers, and is the story VCs want getting truer?

## Watch every week
- Competitor and acquirer moves (Protege, Human Native/Cloudflare, synthetic-data players).
- Regulation that creates buyer demand (EU AI Act GPAI enforcement, California AB 2013, copyright rulings).
- Our own numbers once live: GMV, take rate, time-to-match, repeat buyers.

## Standing questions for Astra
1. Which one vertical (one data type, one buyer type) are we proving first?
2. Which named buyer teams did we talk to this week, and what did they say they'd pay?
3. Partnership: are roles, time commitments and the founder agreement settled?
4. Raise now, or after three paying pilots?

## Output
Section `## CEO` in `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: three "pay attention" bullets with sources, then the open questions.
