## Where to start
Pick one vertical where (a) buyers face disclosure pressure, (b) data is owned by individuals who can attest to it, and (c) video/multimodal giants aren't already dominant. Candidates to test in discovery calls: domain-expert Q&A (professionals answering bounties), customer-support conversations from small businesses, and voice/accent data with explicit consent. Decide after 10 calls, not before.
